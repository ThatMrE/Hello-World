HALF BAD started in 2017 in the back room of a Coventry pub that has since become a letting agency. Eight people turned up to a jam advertised for four. Nobody left.

Since then: four records, two vans (RIP the first), a Reading slot at 11:40am that nobody expected to be full, and a reputation for playing longer than the curfew allows. The horns are loud, the organ is louder, and the whole thing is about three per cent more sincere than it lets on.
